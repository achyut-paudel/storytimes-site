Storytimes

Storytimes is a small publishing tool for the storytelling page storytimes.interest. It shares short narrated videos of folk tales, legends and classic short stories from Nepal, India and around the world.

The tool prepares each video (illustrations, narration and captions) and publishes it to the page's own Instagram and TikTok accounts. Videos are labelled as AI-generated content.

It is used only by the page owner to post to the owner's own accounts.
