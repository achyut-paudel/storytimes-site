Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Storytimes is a personal publishing tool used by the owner of the storytimes.interest page to post videos to the owner's own Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What we access

When the owner connects a TikTok account, Storytimes receives an access token and the account's basic profile information (open ID, display name and avatar) from TikTok. It uses them only to upload or publish videos the owner has made, to that same account.

What we store

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file readable only by the owner. They are never shared, sold or sent to anyone other than TikTok and Instagram.

Viewers

Storytimes does not collect any information about people who watch, like or comment on the videos. Public engagement numbers shown by Instagram or TikTok may be read to learn which stories do well.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect Storytimes at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions. Stored tokens are then deleted.

Contact

Send a direct message to @storytimes.interest on Instagram.
